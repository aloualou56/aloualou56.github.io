$ cat about.md

I'm Elias, a second-year student at the International Hellenic University (IHU) in Serres, ΔΙΠΑΕ Σερρών. My department is Computer, Informatics and Telecommunications Engineering (Τμήμα Μηχανικών Πληροφορικής, Υπολογιστών και Τηλεπικοινωνιών), and I enjoy programming in my free time.

I'm also a teacher in the Robotic Sports department of M.G.S. Panserraikos in Serres (Τμήμα Αθλητικής Ρομποτικής), which describes itself as the first official athletic robotics department in Greece. There I teach the younger generations.

Most of what's on this page started as something I wanted to use: a game to play on my phone, a booking system for small businesses, a little screen that shows the time and the weather.

I work in Python, Kotlin, Java, C++ and JavaScript, and I pick whichever fits the job. A few projects exist twice on purpose. JavaGame was rebuilt in C++ so it runs on Windows and Android, and Nebula Requiem began as a single HTML file before I rewrote it natively in Kotlin.

I write READMEs in English, and some in Greek as well.

./games

Bullet patterns, collision, procedural worlds and the maths behind them.

 ./web-apps

Booking and school management systems, both built with Django.

 ./hardware

E-paper displays, an ESP32 monitor and an NFC reader app.
